## General

Protecting your data matters to me. This privacy policy explains what data is processed when you visit this website, for what purpose, and on what legal basis.

## Server and hosting

This website runs on a server. Technical access data may be processed in the process, for example IP address, date and time of access, page requested, browser type and operating system. This is based on the legitimate interest in a secure and stable operation of the website.

The VPS used is provided by Hostinger and is located in Germany. The specific retention period for server logs depends on the hosting provider's and the website's configuration.

## Plausible Analytics

This website uses Plausible Analytics, Community Edition. Plausible is self-hosted and runs on a dedicated VPS in Germany, provided by Hostinger.

Plausible does not use cookies and does not create personal user profiles. This is based on the legitimate interest in a privacy-friendly, statistical evaluation of website usage in order to improve the website.

## Contact form

The contact form on this website uses the Tally service (Tally, Belgium). The form is embedded as an iframe; Tally processes the data entered directly on its own infrastructure, hosted within the EU.

When you use the contact form, your name, email address and message are processed. This is based on processing your request, where applicable as a pre-contractual measure.

I am additionally notified automatically about new form submissions in an internal Slack workspace, so I can respond promptly. No content from the form (name, email address, message) is transmitted to Slack — only the notice that a new submission has come in.

Data is retained only for as long as necessary to process the request, or as long as legal retention obligations apply.
