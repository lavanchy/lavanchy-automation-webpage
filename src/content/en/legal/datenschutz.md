## 2. General

The protection of your data is important to me. This privacy policy explains which data is processed when you visit this website and within automation and consulting projects, for what purpose and on what basis. It also informs about the rights of persons whose data is processed.

The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) applies. Insofar as personal data of persons from the EU is processed, the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies in addition.

Technical and organisational measures have been implemented to ensure the most complete possible protection of personal data processed via this website. Nevertheless, internet-based data transmissions can in principle have security gaps, so absolute protection cannot be guaranteed. For this reason, every data subject is free to transmit personal data by alternative means, for example in writing or by telephone.

## 3. Categories of data processed

The personal data processed is that which is required to provide the offering in a lasting, user-friendly, secure and reliable manner. Such personal data may in particular include the following categories:

- Master and contact data (e.g. name, email address, phone number)
- Browser and device data (e.g. IP address, browser type, operating system)
- Usage data (e.g. pages visited, time of access)
- Contract and payment data within client projects

Personal data is processed for the period required for the respective purpose or by law. Data whose processing is no longer required is anonymised or deleted (see also section 11).

## 5. Plausible Analytics

This website uses Plausible Analytics Community Edition for the statistical analysis of website usage. Plausible is self-hosted and runs on a dedicated VPS in Germany (Hostinger).

Plausible does not use cookies, does not store IP addresses permanently and does not create personal user profiles. There is no tracking across websites or devices. The basis is the legitimate interest in privacy-friendly statistical analysis of website usage in order to improve the website (Art. 31 para. 1 FADP or Art. 6 para. 1 lit. f GDPR).

## 14. Rights of data subjects

Where the statutory requirements are met, data subjects have the following rights:

- Right of access: to request information about the personal data processed, in particular about the purposes of processing, the categories of personal data, the categories of recipients, the planned retention period, the existence of a right to rectification, erasure, restriction of processing or objection, the existence of a right to lodge a complaint and the origin of the data, if it was not collected directly.
- Right to rectification: to request the rectification of inaccurate or the completion of stored personal data.
- Right to erasure: to request the erasure of stored personal data, unless processing is necessary for exercising the right of freedom of expression and information, for compliance with a legal obligation, for reasons of public interest or for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
- Right to restriction of processing: to request the restriction of processing where the accuracy of the data is contested, the processing is unlawful but erasure is opposed, the data is no longer needed but is still required for the establishment of legal claims, or an objection to processing has been lodged.
- Right to data portability: to receive personal data one has provided in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format or to request its transmission to another controller.
- Right to review of automated individual decisions: in the case of exclusively automated decisions, to present one's own point of view and to request that the decision be reviewed by a natural person.
- Right to lodge a complaint: to lodge a complaint with the competent supervisory authority.

The competent supervisory authority in Switzerland is the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC); in the EU, it is the respective competent data protection authority.
